## 2026-05-21 Advisory Board meeting 

What follows is a brief summary of the topics discussed at the W3C Advisory Board meeting on 2026-05-21

attending: Hidde de Vries, Wei Ding, Emma Fraser, Max Gendler, Tatsuya Igarashi,  Elena Lape, An Qi (Angel) Li, Theresa O'Conner,  Hiroshi Ota, Avneesh Singh, Amy van der Hiel, Song Xu, Brent Zundel

* **Infoshare**
 Amy gave a Team update including on workshops, that W3C was named on the Forbes Accessibility 200 list, and the recent Japan Member meeting and W3C@30 in Japan celebration.

* **May member meeting recap**
The AB recapped the three sessions of the May Monthly Member meeting including very interesting updates from Brent and others on discussions at SDOs including Linux Foundation Open Source Summit, IETF, and the Metaverse standards forum; a retrospective of the AC in China; the recent AB and TAG elections; what could be done to make W3C faster, WGs and charters more efficient, and the W3C more transparent; and the DISPATCH proposal. DingWei and Angel emphasized that the information sharing practice with other SDOs got a lot of positive interest from Members. The AB discussed possible trends in the recent elections and was interested to get more data points about the issue and suggested this would be a good topic for the F2F. 

* **Open Issue Review & Triage**
Brent led discussion of Open Issue review. Issues which were discussed and actioned for follow up included: Issue #169, Insurance for councils. Issues which were discussed and closed included: Issue #75 Community Group Work Items, Issues #217 and 517 on Related Members, and Issue #227 on Dashboard/Homepage. Issues added for discussion on a future agenda included: Issue #219 on Explainers and Issue #230 on CGs and Incubation.
