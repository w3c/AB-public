## 2026-04-16 Advisory Board meeting

What follows is a brief summary of the topics discussed in the W3C Advisory Board meeting on 2026-04-16

attending: Daniel Appelquist, Hidde de Vries, Wei Ding, Emma Fraser, Max Gendler, Tatsuya Igarashi, Elena Lape, An Qi (Angel) Li, Theresa O'Conner,  Hiroshi Ota, Avneesh Singh, Amy van der Hiel, Song Xu, Brent Zundel


* **Info Share**
Amy noted a new member and noted an AB member was lauded as encouraging them to join. The AB discussed nominations for the upcoming AB election and determined to encourage AC to nominate people for election at the AC meeting. 

* **Open issues**
The AB reviewed open issues and discussed which might be investigated, closed or tagged to address at the F2F.  Issues to close included: abstentions in AB/TAG elections and CGs transitioning work including IPR to Standardizing Groups;  Issues for follow up included: communication from the Solid CG. Issues for discussion at the F2F included: implementation experience with fewer browser engines; Membership fees and casual contributors; W3C workshops model; "How to get involved"; Improving the Ambassadors Program; per-day TPAC fees; and the number of IEs. 


* **Agenda Review for F2F in Hangzhou**
The AB reviewed the agenda for the F2F meeting. Tess and Brent agreed to add updates of Process CG issues to the agenda. Dan noted agenda topics including: feedback on the AC meeting, a post-mortem on the ac-poll process; position statements; incubation of new work; Strategic Initiatives; and a presentation from Naomi on Membership restructuring. 


* **Hangzhou Logistics Q&A**
Angel gave an overview of some logistics for the AC and AB meetings in Hangzhou including: side events, locations, and proposed social events. She offered to be available for AB over the weekend for any questions. The AB discussed their travel and preparations.
