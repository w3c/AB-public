## 2026-05-14 Advisory Board meeting 

What follows is a brief summary of the topics discussed at the W3C Advisory Board meeting on 2026-05-14

attending: Daniel Appelquist, Hidde de Vries, Wei Ding, Max Gendler, Tatsuya Igarashi,  An Qi (Angel) Li, Theresa O'Conner, Avneesh Singh, Amy van der Hiel, Andrew Wafaa, Song Xu, Brent Zundel

* **Infoshare**
Amy noted Alex and PLH wrote to say they would reserve time at the TPAC for the AB's DISPATCH experiment. Hidde proposed a potential Meetup around the AB F2F in the NL. The AB supported the idea, discussed logistics, suggested the focus be on a developer meetup and noted it would be important to set aside time after the talks for informal discussions. Song mentioned contacts he'd made at the Global OS Summit in Paris and discussions with representatives from the Linux Foundation on Agentic AI at W3C. Andrew mentioned he would be happy to be a conduit between the groups as he is involved in the Linux Foundation AI group. 

* **Open Issue Review & Triage**
Brent led Open Issue review and triage. Issues reviewed and  actioned for more information included Issue #262 on the IE travel fund and Issue #271 on IEs in WG. Actions listed for discussion at the F2F included Issue #271 on how to get involved in W3C. 

* **May Member meeting agenda building**
The AB discussed the agenda for the May Monthly Member Meeting. Angel suggested an AC meeting recap and several issues that had been raised on ac-forum. DingWei suggested Brent and others might give updates about the IETF and other SDOs.  

* **AB discussion on Tech strategy**
The AB touched on the presentation the previous week from PLH on the technology strategy. Dan gave an overview and noted if anyone had feedback he'd be willing to gather it to take it back to PLH. 

* **TPAC registration fee by day**
The AB discussed the question of per-day fees at TPAC. Amy noted that Alex is happy to join an AB meeting in June to discuss this more but she got an update from the Events Team that they noted they won't be able to change the fees for 2026 because they need a reliable forecast for fees this year. She did note that the Team is working to find options that meet the needs that are being expressed for allowing important attendance. The AB noted the Team shouldn't have to worry about enforcing attendance and expressed their strong concern for flexibility and the importance of being able to bring in interested parties.
