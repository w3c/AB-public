## 2026-05-07 Advisory Board meeting 

What follows is a brief summary of the topics discussed at the W3C Advisory Board meeting on 2026-05-07

attending: Daniel Appelquist, Hidde de Vries, Wei Ding, Seth Dobbs, Emma Fraser, Max Gendler, Tatsuya Igarashi, Elena Lape, Philippe Le Hegaret, An Qi (Angel) Li, Theresa O'Conner,  Hiroshi Ota, Avneesh Singh, Amy van der Hiel, Song Xu, Brent Zundel

* **Infoshare**
The AB discussed the recent AB nomination process which, because it closed with more than 3 but fewer than 5 nominees for 5 seats, resulted in nominees being seated with no voting election. While the AB who were nominated were pleased to be continuing on the AB (and to be welcoming Andrew), they did note some concerns and that not having an election was not their preferred outcome. They discussed a similar case with a recent TAG election and the history around the current Process for elections which does not allow for extension by the Team of nomination periods. Amy also raised a request from Alex for the AB to discuss their goals for a request for TPAC day passes in future meetings.

* **Open Issue Review**
The AB reviewed Open Issues in the GH. Issues reviewed with follow-up included: ac-polling to-dos, per-day fees at TPAC,  and Membership fees and casual contributors. Closed issues included: AC 2026.

* **Philippe Presentation**
Philippe joined the AB to give updates on the Technical Strategy TF and shared slides he presented to the Board in February. He gave an overview including: technical and operational criteria for new technologies as well as principles; considerations of both staff and Member time; why long term commitments might be chosen over what might be most "successful"; and identifying concrete topics for W3C to focus on for the next years. Dan agreed on the importance of the operational as well as ethical criteria and adding questioning whether new technologies were "of the web". 

* **time with Seth**
Time was set aside for Seth to get feedback from the AB and hear questions. Angel noted one question the AB had was on the Structural Evolution TF. Seth gave updates on the TFs noting three of them will be moving from TF to a more long  term structure: Impact Framework, Technology Strategy and Policy. Stakeholder Outreach would continue its work and the OSS proposal would be moved out of the Structural Evolution TF to its own space and the TF would continue and refocus on core structure. The AB asked how they could contribute. Seth noted that he appreciates the AB participation in the TFs. He discussed the importance of having the right members, fees, reducing friction for joining and participating and other work of the Membership TF. Avneesh asked about short term issues for which Seth might like advice and Seth said he'd continue to get on the AB calendar to discuss.
