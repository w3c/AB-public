
## 2026-04-09 Advisory Board Meeting

What follows is a brief summary of the topics discussed in the W3C Advisory Board meeting on 2026-04-09

attending: Daniel Appelquist, Wei Ding,  Emma Fraser, Max Gendler, Tatsuya Igarashi, Elena Lape, An Qi (Angel) Li, Theresa O'Connor, Hiroshi Ota, Avneesh Singh, Amy van der Hiel, Song Xu


* **Info Share**
Dan gave an overview of the recent AB, TAG, BoD Chairs meeting with Seth. Topics included: charters, using AI For TAG reviews, and plans for a joint dinner at the AC meeting. Amy gave a Team update including:  work on EPUB submission to ISO, a reminder to register for the AC meeting, and Team discussions of increasing attempts to submit AI work on lists and to GH. The Team asked for AB guidance on next steps for proposals for AI use in our work. The AB agreed the topic deserves longer discussion and Dan confirmed the topic is on the agenda for the F2F.  The AB also confirmed that the Team should go ahead with the first ac-poll. Avneesh noted that the EPUB submission to ISO was complex and it would be good to set up a coordination meeting. 

* **Planning for AC2025**
The group discussed plans for the AB presentation at the AC meeting to include: who the AB are, position statements, and Priorities. They also proposed an AB Breakout Session. 


* **Agenda Building for F2F in Hangzhou**
The AB reviewed the draft agenda for the AB F2F after the AC meeting, topics included: Feedback on the AC meeting, Position Statements, Incubation of New Work, and Process refactoring.
