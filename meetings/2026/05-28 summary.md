## 2026-05-28 Advisory Board meeting 

What follows is a brief summary of the topics discussed at the W3C Advisory Board meeting on 2026-05-28

attending: Hidde de Vries, Wei Ding, Emma Fraser, Max Gendler, Tatsuya Igarashi,  Elena Lape, An Qi (Angel) Li, Theresa O'Conner,  Hiroshi Ota, Florian Rivoal, Avneesh Singh, Amy van der Hiel, Léonie Watson, Song Xu, Brent Zundel

* **Info Share**
Léonie and Florian of the BoD joined the AB call. Léonie talked about the announcement of the leadership transition that Seth had resigned and Dom would be Interim CEO. The group discussed some details of the transition, the timeframe and the AB asked how they could help. Florian and Léonie encouraged coordinated communications and encouraging people to look to the future. 

Hidde also gave an update on the meetup in Rotterdam. 

* **AB election and transition related topics**
The AB discussed a mail about the AB election. There was a misunderstanding that there was an empty seat on the AB after the last election but the AB discussed posotive other ways in which members could participate including in the Process CG. 

The AB also discussed timing for access to the AB mail and GH for new and outgoing AB members. The AB  was cautious to avoid overlapping ABs for too long but acknowledged that a "warm up" period for new AB members is helpful. The AB resolved that access for outgoing and incoming AB be extended until the end of the transition meeting. 

* **AB meeting plan during TPAC**
The AB resolved to again to formally meet on Monday AM and Friday PM at TPAC. The AB was also interested in attending the New Member Intro and also to less formal welcome events.
